This task force establishes the foundational context for the Working Group by addressing motivation, scope, and reference case studies. While established engineering practices often rely on historical climate records and the  assumption of stationarity, the reality of a changing climate introduces environmental actions that can no longer be treated as constant over the service life of a structure. We aim to present where existing reliability frameworks fall short, and to delineate the specific climate variables, structural systems, and adaptation strategies that define this new engineering reality. Our work follows three steps: (1) a review of existing codes and standards; (2) a structured definition of scope along climate variables, structure types, and uncertainties; and (3) the selection of reference case studies.
