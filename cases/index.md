Subgroup 1 (SG1) of Working Group 1 addresses motivation, scope, and case studies. It aims to establish a shared technical basis for the Task Force by articulating why the existing framework for structural reliability is insufficient in the face of climate change, delineating what will be addressed in terms of climate variables, structural systems, and failure mechanisms, and proposing a set of reference case studies to be used as common benchmarks by the other subgroups and working groups. The work of SG1 is organised into three steps: (1) a review of existing codes and standards; (2) a structured definition of scope along climate variables, structure types, and tipping points; and (3) the selection of reference case studies.
