This task force aims to establish a common methodological basis for the stochastic modelling of non-stationary environmental actions and structural performance under climate change. 

It aims to answer the question of what engineers need in order to model uncertain climate futures in practice, and how this information can be extracted from climate change projections. 

Specifically, the task force will address the following questions: (i) Which stochastic modelling approaches are suitable for representing non-stationary environmental loading behaviour and resistance parameters over the service life of structures? (ii) What information from climate change projections is needed to support these modelling approaches? (iii) What uncertainties are associated with this information, and how should they be represented in the stochastic modelling? The ultimate objective is to provide a common framework that supports communication between climate scientists and engineers and promotes a consistent treatment of load modelling under climate change in structural assessment and design.
